3. What data we process

We only collect what we need to run a weekly-match dating service. Depending on how you use Sundae, this includes:

Account Email address and a securely hashed password (handled by our authentication provider), account creation date, language preference, and whether you have accepted the terms.
Profile First name, age, gender, who you want to meet, a profile photo, and — if you add them — additional photos, a short bio, prompt answers, height, interests, relationship intentions, relationship style, and whether you have or want children.
Personality test Your answers to the one-time personality questionnaire and the personality profile calculated from them. This is used for matching and for your personal insights.
Location If you grant permission, your approximate coordinates and the city derived from them, plus your chosen search radius. Used to match you with people nearby. You can use the app without precise location by entering a city.
Matches & messages Records of who you were matched with and when, the compatibility score, match status, and the messages you exchange with a match.
Feedback & traits The answers you give after a match ends (how it went, selected traits, whether you'd meet again, what happened afterwards), and the aggregated “traits people notice” shown on your profile and to your matches.
Safety & moderation Blocks you create, and reports you submit or that are submitted about you, including the reason and related match. We keep this to operate the service safely and to handle abuse.
Subscription Whether Sundae+ is active for your account and when it started. Payment itself is processed by Apple or Google — we do not receive your card details.
Device & notifications If you enable push notifications, a push token for your device and your notification preference. Basic technical data (app version, platform) may be included in error logs.
Support The content of emails you send us and our replies.
Website Server logs kept by our hosting provider (see section 14) and the email address you enter into the waitlist form, which is sent to us by your own email app.

We do not use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics that build a profile of you.

5. Sensitive data and consent

By its nature, a dating profile can reveal information that the GDPR treats as a special category (Art. 9) — in particular data concerning your sex life or sexual orientation, which may be inferred from the gender you want to meet, and any health, religious or similar details you choose to write in your bio or prompts.

We process this data solely to provide the matching service you signed up for, on the basis of your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You give this consent when you create your profile and enter Sundae. You can withdraw it at any time by editing or removing the relevant fields, or by deleting your account — this does not affect processing that happened before withdrawal.

Please only put information in free-text fields (bio, prompts, messages) that you are comfortable sharing with a match.

9. How matching works

Once per weekly cycle, Sundae selects one candidate for you. The selection uses your personality profile, your stated preferences (who you want to meet, age range, distance), your location, and signals from your past matches and feedback. A compatibility score is calculated and shown to you.

This is an automated selection, but it does not produce a legal or similarly significant effect on you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR — it simply proposes a person to talk to, and you are free to end any match. If you would like a human to review how a match was generated, contact us.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data erased (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing in certain cases (Art. 18);
  • receive certain data in a portable, machine-readable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21);
  • withdraw any consent you have given, at any time, without affecting processing that already took place (Art. 7(3)).

To exercise any of these rights, email support@sundae.pro. We may ask you to confirm the email address on your account so we can be sure it's you. We respond within one month.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account. This ends any active match, removes your profile and photos, and deletes or anonymises your data as described in section 8. Some minimised safety records may be retained as set out above. You can also ask us to delete your account by email.

12. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS). Access to stored data is protected by database access rules that limit what each account can read and write, and by restricted administrative access. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes by our authentication provider. No online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify you and the authorities if a breach ever legally requires it.

13. Age limit

Sundae is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly process data of anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we will delete it.
