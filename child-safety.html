Safety

Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Applies to the Sundae mobile app on all platforms.

Zero tolerance. Sundae has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form — content, behavior, or an account belonging to a minor. Any account or content in violation is removed, the account is permanently and irreversibly blocked, and where required by law we report it to the relevant authorities.

Our commitment

Sundae is an adult dating app. We do not permit any content or behavior that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors, and we do not permit minors to use the app at all. This standard applies everywhere in the app: profiles, photos, prompts, chat messages, voice messages, and postcards.

Age requirement

Sundae is rated 18+ and restricted to adults. Every account must provide a date of birth during sign-up, and the app hard-blocks account creation for anyone under 18 at that step — there is no way to continue past it while underage. We act immediately on any report or other indication that an account belongs to a minor, including permanently removing the account.

How to report a concern

Every conversation in Sundae — chat with a match and postcard conversations — has a Report option directly on that person's profile or conversation. Reporting a person also blocks them immediately and permanently; blocking cannot be undone.

  1. 1Open the conversation or profile of the person you want to report.
  2. 2Tap Report and choose a reason.
  3. 3Add any details that would help our review. Submitting the report also blocks the person right away.

If you're not able to use the in-app report flow, or you want to report a concern about child safety specifically, email support@sundae.pro — include as much detail as you can (the person's name in the app, and screenshots if you have them).

What happens after a report

Reports are reviewed by a human. When a report is filed, we keep a snapshot of the relevant conversation so it can be reviewed — this is the only reason that content is retained beyond its normal lifecycle. An account found to have shared or solicited CSAE content, or to belong to a minor, is permanently banned; this is irreversible and separate from an ordinary block between two users.

Working with authorities

We comply with applicable child-protection laws in every market Sundae operates in. Where we identify or are made aware of CSAE content or behavior, we report it to the relevant national or regional authority (such as NCMEC in the United States, or the equivalent body elsewhere), and we cooperate with law enforcement investigations to the extent required by law.

Contact

The person responsible for child-safety compliance at Sundae can be reached at support@sundae.pro. This includes questions from platforms, regulators, or law enforcement about our CSAE prevention methods and policy compliance. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
