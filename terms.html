4. The service

Sundae is a dating service with a deliberately limited design:

  • You complete a one-time personality test and set your preferences. Your location is required, because matching is based on who is near you.
  • Once per weekly cycle, Sundae introduces you to one other member selected automatically based on personality, preferences, distance and signals from your past matches. The main introduction is on Sunday; if you were not matched, Sundae may try again once mid-week.
  • Each match runs for a limited period (up to about one week, and no longer than the current cycle). Either person can end it earlier. When it ends, you may be asked for brief feedback.
  • A match's photo is shown blurred at first and is revealed gradually as the conversation progresses. In the chat you can send text messages and voice messages of up to two minutes, and see when the other person is typing.
  • While you are matched, your match can see your full profile text (bio, prompts, the basics you filled in and interests) and the personality profile Sundae derives from your test (as compatibility indicators, not your individual answers). With Sundae+, they can also see your further photos beyond the main one, and later see you in a history of past matches.
  • Discover & postcards: in addition to your match, the app shows a daily selection of twelve people near you who fit your preferences and are not currently matched with you, refreshed once a day. You can send any of them a short postcard (a brief note, delivered the next day); Sundae+ lets you send more per day and reserves you a spot in an inbox that's otherwise full. If two people exchange enough postcards and both agree, they can arrange a guaranteed match for the next Sunday, entirely outside the normal matching. Sending or receiving a postcard also becomes one of the signals our matching model considers for future drops. There is no endless feed, no swiping, and no way to search for a specific person directly.

We do not guarantee that you will receive a match in any given cycle, that a match will reply, that any connection will result, or that other members' information is accurate. Matching logic, timing and features may change over time.
