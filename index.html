Questions

Good to know.

What if I don't get a match one Sunday?

It happens, especially early on or in smaller cities. Sundae keeps looking, tries again mid-week with a slightly wider range, and introduces someone as soon as there's a good fit.

Can I browse other people?

Not in the swipe-and-scroll sense. Alongside your one match, Discover shows twelve people near you, refreshed daily — the same twelve for everyone. Send any of them a short postcard; write back and forth enough and you can both agree to a guaranteed match next Sunday.

When is the photo revealed?

Gradually, once both of you have sent a few messages — or after a set amount of time. It's designed so appearance isn't the first thing you react to.

Is Sundae free?

Yes. Matching, chatting, your daily Discover selection and one postcard a day are free. Sundae+ (6,99 € / month) adds optional depth — more postcards, every match photo, deeper insights.

What data does Sundae collect?

The essentials to match you: your verified phone number, profile, personality answers, location and your feedback. Politics and religion are optional. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
