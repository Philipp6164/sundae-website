Why Sundae

Dating shouldn't feel like shopping.

Modern dating apps give you more and more choices — and somehow make it harder to actually get to know someone. Profiles judged in two seconds. Conversations that go nowhere. A feed that never ends.

Sundae takes the opposite approach. One match a week. Personality before photos. And enough time to see where something could go.

How it works

One week.
One person.

You take a short personality test once. After that, Sundae does the choosing — and gives you something rare in dating: time.

01 — SUNDAY

Your match arrives

Every Sunday, Sundae introduces you to one person chosen for you — based on your personality, your preferences and how close you are.

02 — TUESDAY

Nobody's left without a chance

No match on Sunday, or it ended early through no fault of yours? Sundae tries again on Tuesday, with priority — a second chance in the same week, not just next Sunday.

03 — DISCOVER & POSTCARDS

Nothing to swipe. Something to write.

No endless feed — just twelve people near you each day. Instead of swiping, you send a short postcard to whoever appeals to you. Write enough back and forth, and you can both agree to a guaranteed match next Sunday.

04 — PERSONALITY

Personality comes first

Their photo starts blurred. You get to know how someone thinks and talks before you know exactly what they look like.

05 — FEEDBACK

Sundae learns

After each match you answer a few quiet questions. Every answer helps the next Sunday land a little closer.

Questions

Good to know.

What if I don't get a match one Sunday?

It happens, especially early on or in smaller cities. Sundae keeps looking, tries again once mid-week with a slightly wider range, and introduces someone as soon as there's a good fit — you don't have to do anything.

Can I get more than one match a week?

No. One match per weekly cycle is the whole idea. If a match ends early, you'll usually wait until the next Sunday.

When is the photo revealed?

Gradually, once both of you have sent a few messages — or after a set amount of time. It's designed so appearance isn't the first thing you react to.

Can I browse other people?

Not in the swipe-and-scroll sense. Alongside your one match, Discover shows twelve people near you who fit your preferences, refreshed daily — the same twelve for everyone. You can send any of them a short postcard; if you write back and forth enough, you can both agree to a guaranteed match for the next Sunday, no algorithm involved.

Is Sundae free?

Yes. Matching, chatting (text and voice messages), your daily Discover selection and one postcard a day, and your core insights are free. Sundae+ (6,99 € / month) adds optional depth — more postcards, every match photo, deeper insights — and helps keep Sundae independent.

What data does Sundae collect?

The essentials to match you: your verified phone number, profile, personality answers, location and your feedback. Politics and religion are optional and only shown if you fill them in. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which platforms is Sundae on?

Sundae is a mobile app for iOS and Android, in English, German and French. Join the waitlist to hear when it opens in your city.
