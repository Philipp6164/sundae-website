5. How we moderate

To keep objectionable content and abusive users off Sundae, we use a combination of measures:

  • Clear rules — the prohibitions in section 4 and in the Terms of Use are binding on every user.
  • In-app reporting — every match conversation has a report control. Reporting a person also immediately blocks them and ends the match.
  • In-app blocking — you can block any match at any time, which ends the match and prevents you being paired again.
  • Human review — we review every report. When a report is filed, the relevant conversation is captured so it can be assessed even if an account is later deleted.
  • Removal and ejection — we remove content that breaks the rules and suspend or permanently ban the account responsible. A banned user is dropped from the service and cannot sign back in.
  • Proactive action — we may review, restrict or remove content and accounts on our own initiative where we reasonably believe the rules have been broken or someone is at risk.

We do not currently run automated scanning of photos or messages. Moderation is complaint-led and manual, backed by the tools above.

6. Reporting and blocking

Report objectionable content or an abusive user

In the app, open the match conversation, use the menu and choose Report, then pick a reason. This sends the report to us, blocks the person and ends the match.

You can also email support@sundae.pro with the details. If the content is on this website or you cannot access the app, email is the right channel.

Block an abusive user

In the match conversation menu, choose Block. The match ends immediately and Sundae will not pair you with that person again. You can review and undo blocks in Settings → Blocked people.
